# Android Developer Technical Challenge: NewsApp Refactoring

## Overview

Engage in our NewsApp Refactoring Challenge to demonstrate your prowess in Android development. The
objective is to enhance and refine NewsApp, emphasizing the application of clean architecture.

## Challenge Details

Your task is to address critical issues and revamp NewsApp, focusing on:

1. **State Preservation**: Ensure the app's state remains constant during screen rotations.
2. **Memory Leak Resolution**: Detect and fix memory leaks to improve app performance.

## Refactoring Goals

- **Best Practices Implementation**: Adopt modern Android development best practices.
- **Scalability and Maintainability**: Ensure the app is easily extendable for future enhancements.
- **UI Differentiation for News Types**: Redesign the UI to distinctly showcase various news
  categories.

## Submission Guidelines

- Access the project using the provided zip file.
- Include a description of any incomplete aspects along with your envisioned approach.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **Code Quality**: Focus on state of the art Android, well-structured, readable, and efficient
  code, with attention to naming and packaging.
- **Adherence to Clean Architecture**: Organize code effectively, aligning with clean architecture
  principles.
- **Problem Solving**: Successfully address and resolve identified issues.
- **Testing**: Develop comprehensive and meaningful tests.

## How We Review Your Submission

Your submission will be reviewed via a pull request in our private repository. 
Several of our developers will take the time to thoroughly evaluate your work based on the criteria outlined above. 
We look at both the code and your reasoning behind key decisions.

After the review, we’ll follow up with you in one of two ways:
- If the submission doesn’t meet our expectations, we’ll share detailed feedback with you.
- If the submission shows strong potential, we’ll invite you to a final interview to discuss your approach and decisions in more depth.

Show us your best.
We look forward to seeing your approach in refining and enhancing the NewsApp. Best of luck.
